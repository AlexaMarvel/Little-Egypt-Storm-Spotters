How to read the radar

Colors show how strongly energy bounces back to the radar dish, which tells you how intense the precipitation is. Spotters fill in what radar cannot see from the ground.

  • Green: Steady light rain
  • Yellow: Moderate rain
  • Orange: Heavy rain
  • Red: Very heavy rain or torrential downpours
  • Magenta: Extreme rainfall, possible hail
